# Report format (A–F)

| Section | Contents | Produced by |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Performance summary** | Top 5 and bottom 3 posts, each with the pattern behind it | competitor-analysis |
| **B. Patterns** | Themes, formats, hooks, timing, caption styles and CTAs that consistently outperform | competitor-analysis |
| **C. Trend report** | Per trend: what it is, example account, why it works, difficulty (Easy/Medium/Hard) for a small phone-shooting team | trend-scouting |
| **D. Content ideas** | 10 ideas: concept, format, hook line, shot list / slide outline, caption draft, CTA, hashtags, source inspiration, with the required mix | content-ideation |
| **E. Gaps & opportunities** | What competitors aren't doing that we could own | content-ideation |
| **F. Risk flags** | Competitor claims/trends not to copy; fixes to our own ideas | claims-check |

Every section labels `[observed]` vs `[inference]` where it matters, and ends with a coverage note (what couldn't be accessed, which conclusions are tentative).
