# Trend scouting

## Inputs
- `brand-profile` (category, city, audience, language mix)
- Optional: seed hashtags, creators or a theme from the user

## Where to look
- Reels feed and Explore, from a normal signed-in viewer
- Category hashtags (e.g. pet food, dog parents), local hashtags (city and neighbourhoods) and adjacent ones (home cooking, food reels)
- Category creators (pet influencers, vets and nutritionists, breed pages) and local creators
- Audio pages for sounds that keep coming up in relevant Reels

## For each trend, record
| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| What it is | Format/template in one line (e.g. "POV: your dog watching you cook their meal") |
| Example account(s) | Real handles you actually saw; link if possible |
| Evidence | What shows it's trending: number of examples seen, view counts if visible, how recent. Label `[observed]` vs `[inference]` |
| Why it works | `[inference]` |
| Fit for the brand | How it maps to our differentiators and audience |
| Difficulty | **Easy / Medium / Hard** for a small team on a phone in our city |
| Risk | Licensed audio, claims, or anything the brand shouldn't do |

## Outputs
- **C. Trend report**, ranked by fit × ease
- Feeds `content-ideation` as source inspiration

## Rules
- Don't present one viral post as a trend; say how many examples you saw.
- Don't make up accounts, view counts or audio names.
- Read-only. Stop on login walls, CAPTCHAs or rate-limit warnings.
