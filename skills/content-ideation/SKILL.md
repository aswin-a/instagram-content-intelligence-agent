# Content ideation

## Inputs
- Patterns (section B), trend report (section C), `brand-profile`
- Optional: a campaign, festival, product launch or offer from the user

## 10 ideas, each with
1. **Concept**: one line
2. **Format**: Reel / carousel / static / Story
3. **Hook line**: first 3 seconds or first line
4. **Shot list** (Reel) or **slide outline** (carousel)
5. **Caption draft**: brand voice; local language touches where the brand profile allows
6. **CTA**: usually the brand's main conversion path (e.g. WhatsApp order, free sample)
7. **Hashtags**: category + local + branded
8. **Source inspiration**: which post or trend it adapts, and what we changed

## Required mix (adjust to the brand profile)
- ≥ 2 life-stage ideas (e.g. puppy, senior)
- ≥ 2 education / trust ideas (e.g. macros, ingredients, kitchen transparency)
- ≥ 2 customer-proof ideas (real customers who've given permission only)
- ≥ 1 breed-specific idea

## Adapt, don't copy
Pull out the *mechanic* (hook structure, format, emotional beat) and rebuild it with our product, voice and differentiators. No reused captions, scripts, audio-plus-visual combos or distinctive visuals.

## E. Gaps and opportunities
What competitors **aren't** doing that we could own, e.g. a local neighbourhood angle, local-language content, live kitchen transparency, macro breakdowns, a focus on local or Indian breeds. Each gap: evidence it's open (`[observed]`) + why it fits us.

## Before output
Run every idea through `claims-check`. Rewrite or drop anything that fails.

## Outputs
- **D. Content ideas** (10)
- **E. Gaps & opportunities**
