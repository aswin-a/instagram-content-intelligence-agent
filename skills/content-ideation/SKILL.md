# Content ideation

## Inputs
- Patterns (section B), trend report (section C), post records (for gap evidence), `brand-profile` (voice, CTA, idea mix, breed examples, gap angles)
- Optional: a campaign, festival, product launch or offer from the team
- **If B or C isn't available in this conversation:** say so, then either run the missing stage or go ahead with brand-led ideas labelled `brand-led, no source`. Never make up a source inspiration.

## 10 ideas, each with
1. **Concept**: one line
2. **Format**: Reel / carousel / static / Story
3. **Hook line**: first 3 seconds or first line
4. **Shot list** (Reel) or **slide outline** (carousel)
5. **Caption draft**: brand voice; local-language touches where the brand profile allows
6. **CTA**: usually the brand's main conversion path (e.g. WhatsApp order, free sample)
7. **Hashtags**: category + local + branded
8. **Source inspiration**: which post (URL) or trend it adapts, and what we changed

Also emit each idea as a row matching [schemas/idea-record.md](../../schemas/idea-record.md).

## Required mix
Use the brand profile's **idea mix**. Default if none is set: ≥ 2 life-stage (e.g. puppy, senior) · ≥ 2 education/trust · ≥ 2 customer-proof (real customers who've given permission only) · ≥ 1 breed-specific (from the brand profile's breed examples).

## Adapt, don't copy
Pull out the *mechanic* (hook structure, format, emotional beat) and rebuild it with our product, voice and differentiators. No reused captions, scripts, audio-plus-visual combinations or distinctive visuals.

## E. Gaps and opportunities
What competitors **aren't** doing that we could own. Start from the brand profile's gap angles (e.g. a neighbourhood angle, local-language content, live kitchen transparency, macro breakdowns, local or Indian breeds). For each gap give the evidence it's open (`[observed]`, from the post records) and why it fits us.

## Gate: claims-check
Every idea goes through `claims-check` (gate mode) **before** it can appear in D. It passes, gets rewritten with the safe alternative, or is dropped and replaced. The final 10 must still meet the mix.

## Outputs
- **D. Content ideas** (10) + idea records
- **E. Gaps & opportunities**

## Stop conditions
- **Done** when 10 ideas have passed the gate and the mix is met. If the mix can't be met (e.g. no customer permission for proof ideas), say which slot is short and why. Don't pad it.
