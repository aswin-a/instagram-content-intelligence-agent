## Notes
- Dog+cat brands (BLEP, Wuuff&Meow, DoggieDabbas): dog content only.
- Look-alike to avoid: @thefarmersdogpub is a UK pub, not The Farmer's Dog.
- Instagram hides likes on most posts: judge Reels by views ÷ followers and views vs the account's own median.
