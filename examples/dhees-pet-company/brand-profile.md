# Brand profile: Dhee's Pet Company (example)

The real configuration the agent was built for. All information here is public, from dhees.com and the brand's Instagram.

## Identity
- Dhee's Pet Company, dhees.com, Instagram @dhees_pet_company
- Fresh **dog** food. **Dogs only**: no cat content, recipes or hashtags.
- Delivers across Chennai and suburbs (55 km radius)

## Product range (all dogs)
- **Balance Chicken Bowl** (Rs 299/kg): everyday energy; chicken, liver, gizzard, egg, pumpkin, carrots, beans, sardines for omega-3
- **Balance Beef Bowl** (Rs 399/kg): higher fat for active dogs
- **Balance Chicken Bowl - Puppy** (Rs 349/kg): soft veggies, higher protein and fat for growing pups
- Every recipe shows calories and its protein/fat/carb split; ingredients are named plainly
- Freshly cooked, small-batch, human-grade; no artificial preservatives, no fillers (corn/wheat/soy); lab-tested; nutritionist-formulated

## Audience
Chennai dog parents who treat their dogs as family and want to move off kibble. Neighbourhoods include Adyar, Anna Nagar, T. Nagar, Velachery, OMR, ECR, Porur, Mylapore, Besant Nagar, Tambaram and more.

## Conversion path
- WhatsApp order, free-sample request, meal-plan consultation
- Social proof: dog parent reviews (e.g. Rottweiler and Beagle owners), "1000+ meals served", "500+ happy pets"

## Tone and language
Warm, trustworthy, nurturing, local to Chennai, science-backed but not clinical. English with natural Tamil/Tanglish touches is fine for Reels and captions.

## Differentiators
Freshly cooked to order · transparent macros · named real ingredients · local delivery · free samples · nutritionist-formulated

## Claims rules
- Never: prevents/treats/cures disease (cancer, allergies, kidney disease, etc.) or guaranteed longer life
- Not without confirmation: "vet approved", "clinically proven"
- Prefer: ingredients, macros, freshness, delivery, real customer results (with permission)
